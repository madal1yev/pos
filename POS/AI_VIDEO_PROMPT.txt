## VISUAL STYLE SPECIFICATIONS

### Phone Mockup
- iPhone 15 Pro style with Dynamic Island (black pill at top center)
- Side buttons visible: 2 on left (volume up/down), 1 on right (power)
- Thin white outline (2px) with subtle outer glow
- Background behind phone: dark indigo gradient, slightly blurred
- Phone frame color: very dark gray (#080812)

### Animations
- Screen transitions: smooth slide-left (0.3s ease-in-out)
- Tap dots: white circle, 90px, appears for 0.5s with pulsating alpha (0.5 → 1.0 → 0.5)
- Floating chips: gentle vertical bobbing (±12px, period 2.4s, sine wave)
- Phone zoom: slow continuous zoom (in or out, ±14% over clip duration)
- Light sweep: translucent white bar (240px wide, 5% opacity) sweeps across background
- Progress bar: thin white line at top (8px height), fills left-to-right across scenes

### Color Palette
- Primary: Indigo #4f46e5 / #6366f1
- Background: Dark #0f172a → #17103f gradient
- Success/Positive: Emerald #6ee7b7
- Warning/Hot: Amber #fbbf24
- Info/Light: Blue #a5b4fc
- Text: White (primary), #c7d2fe (secondary), #818cf8 (accent)

### Typography (for text overlays)
- Font: Inter or SF Pro Display (bold weight)
- Title size: 170–185px
- Caption size: 50–55px
- Chip size: 28–34px
- Color: Always high contrast against dark background

## MUSIC SPECIFICATIONS

Genre: Warm corporate ambient / Soft electronic
Tempo: 90–100 BPM (calm, confident, not rushed)
Mood: Professional, trustworthy, modern, optimistic

Structure:
- 0:00–0:06: Soft ambient pads enter (Am chord), single pluck notes
- 0:06–0:15: Pluck melody develops, soft kick enters, energy gently builds
- 0:15–0:21: Full arrangement — pads + plucks + kick + bass, warm and full
- 0:21–0:27: Gentle resolution, final chord rings out, clean ending

Instrumentation:
- Warm synthesizer pads (low-pass filtered, detuned slightly)
- Pentatonic pluck synth (C major pentatonic: C-D-E-G-A)
- Soft kick drum (low-pass, not punchy — warm and round)
- Sub bass (follows chord roots: A → F → C → G)
- NO drums beyond kick (no hi-hats, no snares, no claps)
- NO vocals, NO speech, NO text-to-speech
- NO harsh sound effects, NO whooshes, NO impacts

Audio quality: Stereo, 48kHz, -16 LUFS integrated loudness
